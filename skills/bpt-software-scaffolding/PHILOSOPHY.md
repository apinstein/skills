# Framework Philosophy

This file establishes the foundational principles driving **this specific** implementation of the AI Pattern Language. It serves as the "soul" of the framework. If another team copies this pattern language but decides to prioritize "engineering purity" over "product market fit," they should rewrite this document.

## 1. Product-First Gravity
Our core thesis is that **a product is a solution to a real problem.** 
- The Product is the center of gravity.
- The Business exists to ensure the Product can sustain itself commercially.
- The Technology exists solely as the mechanism by which the Product is delivered to the user.

Therefore, when an AI agent faces a structural conflict (e.g., "The easiest technical architecture creates a worse UX"), the tension must be resolved in favor of the Product, unless it poses catastrophic Business or Technical failure.
